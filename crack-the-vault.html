🛡️ What the vault taught us

🔑
Use long, random passwords — not predictable patterns (like the sequences you just solved!).
🔁
Never reuse the same password across sites.
🧩
Codes and ciphers are just patterns — and predictable human passwords are patterns too, which is why they're easy to crack.
📱
Enable two-factor authentication where possible.
